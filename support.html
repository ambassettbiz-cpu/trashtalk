Trash Talk Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email ambassett@verizon.net. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you tapped just before the problem. A screenshot helps.

How do I learn a game?

Open the game from the home screen and tap How to Play. Each game has its own rules page.

How do I play online with a friend?

Both of you need to be signed in to Game Center (iPhone Settings → Game Center). Then tap Play Online, invite your friend, and take turns. You'll get a notification when it's your turn.

Why is a game locked?

Junkyard Ballpark, Can It, Pass the Trash, Dumpster Fire and Garbage Chute unlock at level 5 or with coins. You earn coins and XP by finishing games.

What is energy?

Each game costs a little energy, which refills over time. Leveling up also fills it to the top.

How do I turn off music or sounds?

Open Settings in the app and switch off Music or Sound effects. The phone's silent switch also mutes the game.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress, coins and saved games.
