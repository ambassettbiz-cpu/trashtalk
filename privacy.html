Trash Talk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Trash Talk is a game app made by Coach Bassett ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. Trash Talk has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, level, settings, saved games and the trash-talk phrases you build are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Online games, player names and turn-based matches use Apple's Game Center. When you play online, Apple handles your Game Center account, nickname and match data under Apple's privacy policy. Trash-talk lines you send go only to the other players in that match.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules turn reminders on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment information.

Children

Trash Talk does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
